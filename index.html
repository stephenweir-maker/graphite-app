Graphite

a paper notebook for your S Pen

Write by hand on your phone or tablet and watch it turn into typed text as you go. It feels like pencil on paper, saves everything on your device, and works offline. It's free, with no account.

Download for Android

Android 8 or newer · about 46 MB · latest build

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone or tablet and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If it asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (it takes you to the setting, flip it on, then go back).
  3. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may say the app isn't known to it, because it's not from the Play Store. Tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open Graphite and connect to the internet once. The handwriting reader downloads (about 20 MB), then it works offline.
Updating: download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your notebooks stay. To be extra safe, use Backup & restore in the app first.

What it does

  • Live handwriting to text, offline
  • Autocorrect: smart fix, underline mistakes, or off
  • Real paper look: lined, grid, dots, planners, music
  • Writing or drawing mode
  • Scribble out to erase, lasso to move and resize
  • Hold still to tidy shapes
  • Hand rest: only the pen writes
  • Sticky notes
  • Pages kept together like a book
  • Search everything you've written
  • Record your voice, tap words to hear that moment
  • Write on PDFs and photos
  • "Remind me to…" becomes a reminder
  • Folders, tags, favourites
  • Backup to Google Drive
  • Teach it your handwriting

Works best with

A phone or tablet with an active pen: Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets with an S Pen, Lenovo tablets with their pen, or Boox e-ink tablets. A plain rubber-tip stylus works like a finger, so palm rejection won't.

Private by design

Your notes never leave your device unless you back them up or share them. Handwriting is read on the device by Google's free on-device reader. Nothing is sent to us.
